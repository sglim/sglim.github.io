It is 2022. A lot happened between the last post and this one. I was CTO of the spinoff for a year, I left, and now I am at Momenti in a different kind of role in a different kind of company. And in all that time I posted almost nothing here.

That is the problem. When I do not write, the things I want stay inside my head and never get properly pulled out. I have a lot of things I want to do. To do even one of them well, I have to keep writing. Not because writing is the goal. Because writing is how I find out what the goal is.

Why did I stop? It is a habit problem. Nothing more dramatic than that. The year as CTO ate the evenings and the habit did not survive.

I found a site recently called 100 Days To Offload. The idea is simple. Write a hundred posts in a year. No other rules. No minimum length, no topic, no schedule beyond the count.

I think that is a good challenge for me, so I am taking it. A hundred is a lot. Roughly one every three and a half days. Some of them will be three sentences. That is allowed. The point is not to write well, it is to write, and let the well part show up on its own after fifty or sixty.

Things I want to write about this year, in no order:

- The player work at Momenti. There is a lot of it and most of it is interesting.
- Learning a new language. Probably Rust, since the engine is Rust and I keep reading it without being able to write it.
- Small tools I build for myself. A crawler, a script, a Dart package. Those posts are easy and I like them.
- Things I read on Hacker News that stuck. I said I would keep a file in 2019. I did not. Trying again.
- Life stuff. Moving, maybe. There is a conversation happening at home about living somewhere English is the first language for a while. Not decided. Worth writing about either way.

Writing my small thoughts now, while they are small, matters more than waiting for them to become big. They do not become big in the drawer. Let's start now.

#100DaysToOffload
