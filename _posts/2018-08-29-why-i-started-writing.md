It is raining hard tonight. This summer was long and hot and the typhoon missed us and now it has been raining for days. Part of the city near my old university flooded. And here I am on the couch with a laptop, writing, which feels like a strange thing to be doing while the weather is doing that.

I want to explain why this blog exists, mostly to myself.

A life that is not recorded is easy to forget. That sounds like something from a poster but I mean it very literally. I tried last month to remember what I worked on in the first half of last year and I could not. I have the time tracking sheet, so I know the categories and the hours. I do not know what I was thinking. I do not know what I was worried about or what I was proud of. The hours are there. The person is gone.

That is embarrassing. I have been doing this job for a while now. Game servers, enterprise software, an internship at a big company, two years at a startup that is becoming a real company. And when I try to tell someone about it, I get the resume version. Bullets. I can't get the texture back.

So this is a place to keep the texture.

I also have not been to the gym in a long time. I have a truck full of reasons. The real one is that I am lazy. Writing is the same. If you don't do it the muscle goes soft and it gets harder to start again. I am starting again.

Rules I'm setting for myself, which I will probably break:

- Write about what I actually did, not what I think sounds smart.
- Short is fine. A paragraph is fine.
- Write when something happens, not at the end of the year when I have forgotten it.
- Korean or English, whichever comes out. Probably English more often, because I want to get better at it and because the people I learn from write in English.

I don't expect anyone to read this. That is not the point. The point is that in three years, when I try to remember what 2018 felt like, there will be something here that is not a spreadsheet.

The rain is still going. Sinchon is still under water. I'm going to bed.
