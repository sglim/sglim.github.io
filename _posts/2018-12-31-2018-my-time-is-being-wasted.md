My time is being wasted. Not by anyone else. By me.

Every day I read light stuff. Social feeds, news portals, the little cards that show up on the phone. And yet the fact that I can sit here tapping this into a phone and publish it is a kind of luxury that this age gives me. Reading is cheap. Writing is cheap. Everything is cheap.

There is so much to consume when you are bored. Almost none of it makes me better. It makes me consume information in the simplest possible form. It goes in, it does not get digested, it goes straight out. And then those undigested bits are what my own writing is made of. No wonder it comes out thin.

There is good writing out there, dense and nutritious. But you have to chew it and sit with it before it becomes yours, and I do not have the patience left over at the end of a day.

Modern life moves fast and I'm part of it, so I run at its speed. That is a kind of responsibility. It is also guilt toward colleagues and toward the parts of me that are not good enough yet. If you run without knowing how fast, eventually running itself becomes the point. Some kind of workplace Stockholm syndrome. The slow craftsman looks inefficient. The factory line looks heroic. You start to worship overtime and you start to quietly judge the ones who go home, and it does not even feel wrong anymore, because the world is moving that fast and there is no time.

That last part is a lie. I do not lack time.

The phone got a screen time feature this year. The numbers are right there. My "no time" is measured in hours per day, and it is not spent on anything I would defend. The good news, if there is any, is that I am embarrassed by it. If you are not embarrassed you are not paying attention.

So it goes around. I resolve to stop wasting time. I forget. I use "the world is fast" as an excuse. And today, again, I read the news fast, read the docs fast, wrote the code fast.

That is why I have to write. Writing is the only thing that makes me look straight at myself. Nothing I write satisfies me when I read it back. At some point I give up and publish anyway. But in that process nothing is wasted. Not the time thinking before I start, not the time actually writing, not the time rereading it and being annoyed at my own vocabulary. None of that is time I can throw away.

So before 2018 ends I wanted to record the waste. Write it down so it exists.

Before 2019 ends I hope the screen time number is one I am not embarrassed by. That is the whole resolution. It is small. I would like to actually keep it.
